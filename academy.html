Trader standard

The 5 rules before capital

  • Know exactly why you are entering.
  • Know where the idea is invalidated.
  • Know the maximum amount you can lose.
  • Know where profit will be taken.
  • Accept that no setup is guaranteed.
Protecting capital gives your edge enough time to work.

Trading psychology: a professional mindset.

Discipline over impulse

Trade the plan, not the candle. If a setup is not valid, staying flat is a correct decision.

  • Write entries before risk is taken.
  • Set daily loss limits.
  • Stop after emotional damage.

Detach from the outcome

A good trade can lose. A bad trade can win. Judge yourself by process, not one outcome.

  • Review the reason behind every entry.
  • Think in series of trades.
  • Focus on execution quality.

Control FOMO

Chasing momentum can ruin process discipline. Wait for clean structure, not desperation.

  • Missed trades are not punishments.
  • Never size up to recover losses.
  • Follow the plan calmly.

Risk management advice.

Risk small enough to think clearly

Choose a maximum risk that you can accept emotionally without compromising your process.

Stop equals invalidation

Place stops where your thesis is invalid. Never move the stop to justify the trade.

Cap daily damage

When losing limits are reached, stop. Protect tomorrow instead of forcing outcomes today.
