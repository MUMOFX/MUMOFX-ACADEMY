TRADER STANDARD

The 5 rules before capital

  • Know exactly why you are entering.
  • Know where the idea is invalidated.
  • Know the maximum amount you can lose.
  • Know where profit will be taken before emotion takes over.
  • Accept that no setup is guaranteed.
Academy principle: Protecting capital gives your edge enough time to work.

Trading psychology: build the professional mindset.

DISCIPLINE OVER IMPULSE

Trade the plan, not the candle. If your setup is not present, staying flat is a valid decision.

  • Use a written entry model.
  • Set a daily loss limit.
  • Stop after repeated emotional decisions.

DETACH FROM THE OUTCOME

A good trade can lose and a bad trade can win. Judge yourself by process quality, not one result.

  • Think in series of trades.
  • Record the reason for every entry.
  • Review execution before P/L.

CONTROL FOMO

If price has already expanded without your planned entry, do not chase simply because it is moving.

  • Wait for your retracement or trigger.
  • Missed trade ≠ lost opportunity forever.
  • Never increase risk to recover a missed move.

RECOVER FROM LOSSES

One loss should not become a revenge session. Reset, review and return only when your decision-making is calm.

  • Respect your loss limit.
  • Take a break after emotional damage.
  • Never double size to “win it back”.

CONFIDENCE WITHOUT EGO

Confidence comes from preparation and repetition. Ego assumes the market owes you an outcome.

  • Stay curious.
  • Allow invalidation.
  • Keep improving your playbook.

CONSISTENCY IS THE EDGE

Professionalism is repeating good behaviour even when the market is boring, fast or unpredictable.

  • Same process.
  • Same risk rules.
  • Same review routine.

Risk management advice.

RISK SMALL ENOUGH TO THINK CLEARLY

Choose a maximum risk per trade that you can emotionally accept. A common educational benchmark is 0.5%–1%, but each trader must determine an appropriate limit.

STOP = INVALIDATION

Place the stop where the trade thesis is invalidated, then calculate position size from that distance rather than forcing the stop to fit a desired lot size.

CAP DAILY DAMAGE

Set a daily loss ceiling and stop trading when it is reached. Protect tomorrow's opportunity instead of trying to rescue today.

WATCH TOTAL EXPOSURE

Several correlated positions can behave like one large trade. Consider combined exposure across gold, USD pairs, indices and crypto.

RESPECT EVENTS & VOLATILITY

Major economic releases can increase spreads, slippage and price speed. Know the event risk before committing capital.

NEVER USE RISK TO CHASE

Do not increase size because of a losing streak, missed entry or desire for a target. Risk should be determined before emotion enters.
